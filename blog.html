#References #Recs #Links

Favorite Resources

CC Search Portal 🡵
Search CC licensed works across multiple platforms.
Ellipsus 🡵
A great free online writing tool. It was a dealbreaker for me that they didn't have offline access, but they're coming out with offline versions soon so I can now reccommend it wholeheartedly!
OneLook Thesaurus 🡵
The easiest way to find a specific word that's just on the tip of your tongue.
Power Thesaurus 🡵
A tool to easily find synonyms, antonyms, examples, etc.
Calibre 🡵
A free e-book manager with tons of great plugins and customization options.
Marginalia Search 🡵
My favorite search engine. Great for falling down niche rabbit holes and finding corners of the internet you never knew existed.
Internet Archive 🡵
Best known as the home of the Wayback Machine, but also hosts a trove of media.
myNoise 🡵
Adjustable, customizable soundscapes. Great for focusing or relaxing.
AdorkaStock 🡵
A great resource for pose references.
Paletton 🡵
A site that makes it easy to create color palettes.
Color Hex 🡵
A lot of color information and tools. Create gradients, see how colors are affected by color blindness, convert to different color systems, etc.
Krita 🡵
A free open source digital painting program. Has some great basic animation tools.
BeepBox 🡵
A very fun online tool for making music.
